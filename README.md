# AI-Orchestrated Campus Microgrid with Solar, Storage, EV and Critical- Load Resilience

An affordable, low-voltage AI-orchestrated solar microgrid designed for autonomous energy management while maintaining a strict, independent safety layer.

The system uses AI to predict energy availability, optimize load scheduling, and detect anomalies, while an ESP32-based controller retains final authority over safety-critical actions.
